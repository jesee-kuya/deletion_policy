Account Deletion Policy

App Name: Voltra

Developer: VoltraKe

How to Request Account Deletion

In the App: open Profile → Policy & Account Deletion → Request Account Deletion. You can cancel the request from the same place until it is processed.

By email: send an email to marcedesglc7@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, and include the phone number registered to your Voltra account.

Before deleting, Voltra contacts you on your registered phone number to confirm that the request really comes from you.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, national ID or passport number, location and profile photo
  • Your registered devices, their details and photos
  • Your marketplace listings
  • Your notifications, push notification tokens and support messages
  • Your sign-in sessions and sign-in codes

Your account record is replaced by an anonymous "Deleted account" entry that no longer contains your name, phone number, ID number or photo.

What Is Kept

  • Payment and payout records, as long as Kenyan tax and accounting law requires
  • Records of stolen-device cases, where needed for a police investigation
  • Records of actions taken by Voltra staff, kept for security and accountability
  • Any other records the law requires us to keep

Deletion Timeframe

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days after we confirm the request with you.
