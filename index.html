Account Deletion Policy

App Name: ELECTIC

Developer: ELECTIC Technologies

How to Request Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their ELECTIC account by sending an email request.

Send an email to: luandeian@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address or phone number associated with your ELECTIC account to help us verify ownership.

What Data Is Deleted

  • User profile information (name, phone number, email)
  • Registered device information
  • Uploaded device photos
  • Device ownership and verification records
  • In-app notifications and messages

Data Retention

Some data may be retained temporarily for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes:

  • Audit or system logs (retained for up to 90 days)
  • Records required by law enforcement or legal obligations, where applicable

After the retention period, all remaining data is permanently deleted.

Deletion Timeframe

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days after verification.
