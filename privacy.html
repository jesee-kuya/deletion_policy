This Privacy Policy explains how VoltraKe ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of the Voltra mobile application ("Voltra" or "the App"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. VoltraKe is the data controller for the App.

1. What Voltra Does

Voltra lets people register their phones and other devices to themselves, check whether a device is registered or reported stolen before buying it, report stolen devices, transfer ownership, and buy and sell devices. Shops and institutions can register devices they own.

3. Device Checks: What Other People See

  • When you check a device, you see whether it is registered or reported stolen. For a device owned by a private person you see only a shortened name and a partly hidden phone number, never their ID number or photo. For a shop or institution you see its business details.
  • When someone checks a device you own that is not reported stolen, you are told it was checked, roughly where (area name) and the checker's first name and last initial. The checker's phone number, ID number, photo and exact location are not shared with you.
  • When someone checks a device that is reported stolen, the owner receives the checker's name, phone number, ID number, photo and the place of the check, so the device can be recovered with the police. These details are also kept on the stolen-device case for Voltra and law-enforcement officers.
  • Ownership transfers: the sender sees the recipient's shortened name to confirm they are sending to the right person.

4. Who We Share Information With

  • Service providers that run parts of Voltra for us and may use the information only for that purpose: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in, with servers in the European Union), Africa's Talking (SMS), Safaricom (M-Pesa payments and payouts), Google Cloud Vision (reading serial numbers and IMEIs from images), Expo and Google Firebase (push notifications) and Sentry (error reports, with servers in the European Union).
  • Law enforcement and authorities when a device is reported stolen, or where the law requires it.
  • Other users, only as described in section 3.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Kenya, including in the European Union.

6. How We Protect Your Information

  • You sign in with a one-time SMS code. Your session is stored in your phone's secure storage and expires unless renewed.
  • Our servers identify you from your signed-in session only, and you can see and change only your own records.
  • Profile photos are stored privately and shown only through links that expire.
  • Every payment is confirmed directly with Safaricom before it is accepted.
  • Voltra staff access is limited by role, requires a second sign-in step, and every sensitive action is recorded with a reason.
  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.
